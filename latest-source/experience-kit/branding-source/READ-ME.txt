CONCEPT
Three people form an upward-growing community, embraced by an open protective shield. The green-white rhythm refers to Nigeria; the gold centre expresses possibility. The mark is original and is a design proposal, not an adopted official identity.

COLOURS
National green #006B45 / RGB 0,107,69
Opportunity gold #D5A63C / RGB 213,166,60
Deep green #123E31 / RGB 18,62,49
Files are RGB. For commercial print, convert using the printer's specified CMYK profile and proof the green and gold.

USAGE
Keep clear space around the mark of at least one central head diameter. Use the standalone symbol below sizes where the full name remains readable. Use the white version on dark green. Do not stretch or add shadows. The presentation slogan is concept copy, not an official agency motto.

NAME AND REFERENCES
The logo follows the existing NSIPA logo's spelling: National Social Investment Programmes Agency. The current website also uses the singular Programme. Typography remains editable for the agency's preferred final wording.
