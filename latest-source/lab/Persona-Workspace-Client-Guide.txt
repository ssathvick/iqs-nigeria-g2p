PRESENT IN THREE MINUTES
1. Registration officer: Open correction, confirm the community, Save correction & resubmit.
2. Open next workspace: Verification officer. Review case, tick three review checks, Approve registration & connect, Confirm approval.
3. Open next workspace: Programme supervisor. Show household progress and Recent activity.
4. Select Citizen / assisted applicant. Show household status and Programme applications.
5. Reset walkthrough to start again.

The walkthrough uses fictional data held only in the current browser tab. Reload or reset returns it to the starting example. Connected status in this walkthrough is simulated. Registration approval does not authorize benefits or payments.
