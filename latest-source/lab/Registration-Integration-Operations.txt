IQS NIGERIA: BENEFICIARY REGISTRATION AND PROGRAMME INTEGRATION
Implemented and tested on 3 October 2026

ENTRY POINTS
Home: https://nigeriag2p.com/
Beneficiary and household registration: https://nigeriag2p.com/registration/
Staff review: https://nigeriag2p.com/registration/login?mode=staff
Existing beneficiary portal: https://beneficiary.nigeriag2p.com/selfservice
Programme Management: https://pbms.nigeriag2p.com/web

BENEFICIARY JOURNEY
1. Open Beneficiary & household registration from the home page.
2. Select Create beneficiary account. Enter a unique username, fictional email,
   first/family names and your own password of at least 10 characters.
3. After identity sign-in, choose Register myself & my household.
4. Enter a household name, state/FCT, fictional community and monthly household
   income. Member 1 is the applicant/head. Add up to seven further members.
5. Confirm synthetic data use and submit for staff review.
6. Wait for approval. After approval and synchronization, the household and its
   people are available in both registries, the portal backend and PBMS.
7. Select an integrated programme and submit its application once.
8. Track Pending review -> Checking eligibility -> Eligible -> Enrolled.
   Not eligible and Rejected are separate possible outcomes.
9. To use the original beneficiary portal, open it and select SIGN IN TO LOCAL
   DEMO, then use the same newly created Keycloak account. The integrated
   programmes and application statuses are available there too.

STAFF JOURNEY
1. Open Registration & enrollment review from the home page.
2. Sign in as the existing staff administrator: username admin, with the
   existing staff password from your private credentials reference.
3. Open Review details for a submitted household. Check members, income,
   geography and the synthetic declaration.
4. Select Approve & synchronize registries, or Return for correction.
5. Confirm Synchronization is complete. A failed backend connection remains
   pending and retries every minute; Retry synchronization is also available.
6. In Programme applications, select Review & run eligibility.
7. Wait for the native PBMS background worker to finish. Refresh the page.
8. If Eligible, select Confirm enrollment. PBMS's final enrollment approval and
   the portal programme membership are both updated. No payment is initiated.

CREATE A CONNECTED PROGRAMME
1. In the staff workspace, expand Create an integrated benefit programme.
2. Enter a unique mnemonic suffix, descriptive name, simulated NGN benefit,
   maximum monthly household income and minimum household member count.
3. Select Create programme & connect portal.
4. The integration creates the PBMS household programme, benefit code,
   enrollment cycle and rules, plus the matching portal programme and form.
5. Refresh the registration workspace and original beneficiary portal to see
   the programme. Native portal form submissions are discovered every minute.

The initial connected programme is Nigeria Household Support - Integrated Demo:
NGN 25,000 simulated benefit; monthly household income <= NGN 100,000;
at least one household member. These are test criteria, not government policy.

SCOPE AND DATA OWNERSHIP
This is an IQS sandbox integration layer added to the installed OpenG2P
components. It is not an upstream feature that was merely switched on.
The registration service owns intake data and its shared IQS-REG-* references.
Staff-approved intake records are synchronized to:
  - Modern registry: individuals, households and household links.
  - Odoo Social Registry: individuals, household groups and memberships.
  - Portal Odoo backend: matching records, identity subject and memberships.
  - PBMS: a dedicated Household Registry adapter, not farmer-shaped fixtures.

Only programmes created through this connected workflow are mapped automatically.
Existing seeded programmes and applications have not been bulk migrated.
Unrelated Odoo edits/imports are not a general bidirectional synchronization feed.
Approved intake profiles are locked; a correction workflow for already approved
registrations is a separate enhancement. Returned registrations can be edited
and submitted again. Account registration does not verify NIN, send SMS/email
verification or automatically approve benefits. Use fictional sandbox data.

SAFETY AND OPERATIONS
Beneficiary signup is enabled only in the beneficiary identity realm.
Only staff users assigned iqs-intake-reviewer can review or enroll; the existing
staff admin has that role. Beneficiary users cannot enter the staff workspace.
OIDC uses authorization code + PKCE, nonce/state checks and signed token checks.
Forms enforce CSRF tokens. Server sessions expire after one hour and cookies are
Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. Credentials remain in private WSL files.
Registry/application retries are serialized across processes and use stable IDs.
Each integrated eligibility job is explicitly scoped to its applicant household.

After a Windows restart, continue to use only Start-IQS-Public-Sandbox.bat.
It starts the existing platform, Cloudflare, registration service and sync timer.
The new services are also enabled in WSL systemd. The PC must remain awake/online.

Diagnostics, run inside Ubuntu as root:
  systemctl is-active iqs-registration.service iqs-registration-sync.timer
  journalctl -u iqs-registration.service -n 40 --no-pager
  journalctl -u iqs-registration-sync.service -n 40 --no-pager
  systemctl start iqs-registration-sync.service
  kubectl -n nigeria logs deploy/pbms-celery-worker --tail=80
  curl https://nigeriag2p.com/registration/health
