NIGERIAG2P HOUSEHOLD GEOGRAPHY - DATA HANDOVER

Purpose: prepare geographic record data for a later dashboard showing individual household locations on a Nigeria map.

CURRENT LIVE COVERAGE
215 individual records and 58 household records in the modern social registry resolve to a zone and state/FCT.
58 households currently lack coordinates. No household points are invented or substituted with state/LGA centroids.
The geographic catalogue contains six geopolitical zones, all 36 states and FCT, and six demonstration LGAs. Ward values are optional reported names. The full national LGA/ward catalogue is not installed.

FIELDS
country_code, zone_id/name, state_id/name, optional lga_id/name, ward, community, household latitude, longitude, reported accuracy_m and coordinate_source.
Coordinate system: WGS84 decimal degrees. GeoJSON positions use [longitude, latitude]. Coordinate source is user_reported, device_gps_reported, or registry_unverified. Coordinates remain unverified household residence information.
The project geographic identifiers are local codes, not official Nigerian P-codes. Join to a selected map boundary dataset using an approved crosswalk. No map boundaries are included in this change.
Zone membership reference: National Bureau of Statistics NNHS 2018, https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/download/839

DATA CAPTURE
The household registration form includes optional LGA, ward, latitude, longitude and accuracy fields.
Use this device location requires a user click and browser permission. Use it only when physically at the household being recorded. Coordinates persist only on form submission.
A state/LGA centre or device located elsewhere must not be recorded as the household point.
Approved profiles retain their existing editing restrictions. Existing household locations require appropriate field collection and the native authorized change workflow. No bulk coordinate import was implemented.
After staff approval and synchronization, household coordinates reach the modern registry. Individual records retain administrative geography without copying household GPS as individual locations.

Summary and point feeds require the existing sandbox reviewer or programme supervisor access. Production jurisdiction/programme restrictions remain to be configured. Exact location data is not part of the public service hub.
The GeoJSON feed includes household identifiers and location metadata without household names, member names, income, contact information or identity numbers. Missing-coordinate households appear in metadata counts and have no map point.

POSTGRESQL VIEWS IN THE REGISTER DATABASE
ng2p_record_geography: dynamic geographic projection of registry records.
ng2p_geography_record_counts: counts grouped by record type, zone, state and known LGA.
ng2p_geography_areas: project geography catalogue and zone mapping.
Native registry rows are not duplicated. Views derive zones from each current administrative code, so existing and future records remain linked to their administrative area.
Counts represent registry records, not confirmed programme beneficiaries. Households and individuals are separate measures and must not be added to count people.
The aggregate CSV is a snapshot for planning. Dashboard refreshes should use the authenticated endpoints or the database views.
