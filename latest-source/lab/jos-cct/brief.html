Jos Family Support CCT Pilot

Start the guided minister demo — eight service stages →

Proposed design and demonstration only. The US$1.5 million envelope is a planning assumption, not funding confirmation. All households and transactions in the demo are fictional. No funds have been transferred.

The decision requested

Endorse preparation of a time-bound education-focused conditional cash-transfer pilot. Nominate the implementing authority and confirm the funding source, geographic scope, benefit rules and readiness gates before real enrolment or payments.

A defined benefit within a fixed envelope

Proposed maximum reach: 4,250 households × US$25 equivalent per month × 12 months. The maximum monthly benefit comprises $20 base support and a $5 attendance-linked top-up, with approved exemptions. The naira amount and exchange-rate basis require approval; no exchange rate is assumed.

Transfer arithmetic: $1,020,000 base + $255,000 maximum top-up = $1,275,000. This assumes operating costs are inside the $1.5 million envelope. Procurement and local affordability assessment may change the proposed reach. Unused top-ups require an explicit reallocation decision.

Resolve “Jos” before selecting households

Jos North/Bassa and Jos South/Jos East are distinct federal constituency references. The demo shows illustrative LGA cohorts in Jos North, Bassa, Jos South and Jos East to explore these options. No option is selected or approved, and no ward boundary or beneficiary residence is inferred. Validate current constituency and administrative mappings before operational use.

Selection, conditions and safeguards

Use authorised social-register access, current vulnerability and residence checks, human review and an accessible appeal route. Assist potentially eligible households missing from the register. Review overlap with existing benefits under approved programme rules.

Approve the attendance threshold only after assessing school availability and verification capacity. Allow exemptions for school closure, illness, disability-related barriers and displacement. The demo retains base support when top-up evidence is pending or unmet; final policy requires authorisation.

Connected native-service demonstration

Open the staff walkthrough or view the public evidence snapshot. Three fictional cases are persisted in the sandbox: JOS-NATIVE-001 has completed native approval, local-bank credit and statement reconciliation; JOS-NATIVE-003 is waiting in AWE review; JOS-NATIVE-002 is untouched for a new walkthrough.

The native flow uses the existing custom registry adapter, PBMS eligibility and entitlement workers, Approval Workflow Engine, signed SPAR mapping, Bridge, local Example Bank and native statement processing. A fixed NGN 25,000 is used solely as a simulator test amount; no USD conversion is implied. Native attendance is synthetic evidence gated by the Jos adapter, not a school-system connection or the production base/top-up rule.

For the minister, first inspect the completed sample and its source identifiers, then open the pending AWE case. Use the untouched sample to demonstrate registration synchronisation, eligibility, enrolment and calculation. Refresh native status between asynchronous steps. Staff actions are audited; native AWE uses the sandbox integration administrator. Do not claim production separation of duties.

Browser-only planning demonstration script

  1. Overview: distinguish the proposed annual target from the synthetic enrolment and payment figures.
  2. Budget: click an allocation and show its purpose and the $1.5 million reconciliation.
  3. Household journey: use “Walk through a sample household”; enrol household 0001, verify sample attendance, approve, submit, confirm receipt and reconcile.
  4. Exceptions: filter failed payments and demonstrate a retry; show an appeal and the school-closure exemption.
  5. Readiness: finish with the decisions needed before a real pilot.

Actions in the browser-only planning mode are simulated and reversible. The separate connected staff mode writes genuine fictional records to the sandbox services and local Example Bank ledger; the route is restricted to the local Example Bank simulator; no real money is transferred. Real operations need separate authorised staff roles, payment-provider integrations and audit controls.

Measure delivery and outcomes separately

Track independently confirmed receipt, timely payments, failed-payment recovery, complaints and cost per household. Collect school-attendance and household-welfare baselines and follow-up measures. No attendance improvement, poverty reduction or causal impact is claimed by the demo.

Indicative implementation sequence

Weeks 1–4: scope, authority, funding, benefit and access agreements. Weeks 5–8: registration readiness, school capacity and payment controls. Weeks 9–12: dry run, independent review and first-cohort gate. Then: 12 monthly benefit cycles, subject to readiness and approvals. These are planning durations, not committed dates.
