Follow the service journey

  1. Beneficiary repository — register and independently review a household. Inspect the connected registry.
  2. Programme management — create programmes, check eligibility and enrol. Test fictional CCT conditionality and release or hold decisions.
  3. Payment account registry — test valid and invalid simulated payment accounts. The installed SPAR API remains private.
  4. G2P Bridge — simulate successful, delayed, failed, reversed and mismatched payments. Native Bridge remains private.
  5. Last mile delivery — save fictional delivery proof offline and synchronise.
  6. Partner management — open the installed partner workspace. Field-agent certification and 50,000-agent scale need separate validation.
  7. Grievance handling — use the case queue below; six intake channels are represented as simulations.
  8. Knowledge management — search guidance and author, independently review, publish and inspect versions.
  9. Public portal — view the four-language demonstration. Open beneficiary self-service.
